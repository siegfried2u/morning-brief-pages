Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

  • The tool uses Google OAuth access to one Gmail account, numaintereseaza@gmail.com, owned by the operator. It reads that account's Sent folder to find the day's digest and sends a copy of it to a private list.
  • It does not access any other Google account, and it reads no message other than the digest and its own status messages.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or stored outside that Gmail account. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets.
  • Recipients can ask to be removed at any time by writing to the address below.
